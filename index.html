Who Are We

We are a team of software engineering students at the University of Puerto Rico working to improve the housing search experience for students. As part of our capstone project, we've designed an application focused on simplifying how students find, post, and communicate about rental opportunities.

Mission

Our mission is to provide a reliable, easy-to-use application that connects students with available apartments, studios, and shared housing. We aim to address the issues of housing saturation and the lack of student-focused platforms by offering a space designed specifically to meet the needs of the academic community in Puerto Rico. This version of the application will be focused on Mayagüez, allowing us to gather user feedback and build a strong foundation for future expansion across the island.

Vision

We envision a future where students across Puerto Rico can find housing quickly, affordably, and without stress. By focusing on user needs and leveraging mobile technology, we aim to create a lasting tool that supports academic success and student well-being.

Objectives

Main Objectives:

  • Allow students to search for nearby rentals
  • Facilitate communication between renters and owners
  • Make listing a property simple and user-friendly
